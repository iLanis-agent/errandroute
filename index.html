ErrandRoute

Six errands, five different closing times, one Saturday. Most people guess the order and lose the day to backtracking or a locked post office door. ErrandRoute checks every possible order and hands you the one that makes the day work.

Plan my route
  • Up to 8 stops: brute-force exact - all 40,320 orders checked, so the answer is the answer.
  • 9 to 60 stops: multi-start nearest-neighbour with 2-opt and relocate improvement, labelled as heuristic.
  • Opening and closing times per stop: the plan waits for open doors and flags days that simply cannot work.
  • Grid mode estimates drive time (straight line x circuity, your speed). Matrix mode takes real minutes from your map app for exact planning.
  • Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
Drive-time estimates are planning aids, not live traffic. Check real closing times before you rely on them.
