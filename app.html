ErrandRoute

Post office closes at 5, bank at 2, pharmacy at 6. Guess the order and you lose an hour, or the errand. Put in your stops and closing times; get the order that makes the day work.

Honesty note: grid mode estimates road time as straight-line distance x a circuity factor (default 1.3) at your average speed. It is not live traffic. For real drive minutes, use matrix mode and paste times from your map app.
